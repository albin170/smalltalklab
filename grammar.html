💡 Grammar Rules

Subject-Verb Agreement
The verb must match the subject in number.
✓ She likes coffee.
✗ She like coffee.
A vs. An
Use "an" before vowel sounds (a, e, i, o, u).
✓ An apple, an hour.
✗ A apple, a hour.
Tense Consistency
Don't mix past and present in the same sentence.
✓ I went to the store and bought bread.
✗ I go to the store and bought bread.
Its vs. It's
"It's" = "it is" or "it has". "Its" = belonging to it.
✓ It's raining. The dog loves its bone.
✗ Its raining. The dog loves it's bone.
Double Negatives
Avoid using two negatives in one clause.
✓ I don't have any money.
✗ I don't have no money.
